ENGINEERING HIGHLIGHTS

Built for the difficult parts of live voice.

The project focuses on timing, trust boundaries, failure isolation, and operational clarity—not a scripted chatbot demo.

Explore the implementation ↗
  • 01
    Full-duplex audio

    Stateful μ-law/PCM conversion and real-time bidirectional streaming.

  • 02
    Authenticated ingress

    Exact-URL signature checks on Twilio webhooks and WebSockets.

  • 03
    Configurable personas

    Versioned prompts and voices loaded from DynamoDB per call.

  • 04
    Bounded pipelines

    Backpressure, queue limits, retries, and idempotent cleanup.

  • 05
    Privacy-aware telemetry

    Structured CloudWatch signals without phone numbers or transcript text.

  • 06
    Public-demo controls

    Caller rate, concurrency, duration, and rolling budget safeguards.

  • 07
    Durable lifecycle

    On-demand DynamoDB state with deterministic terminal outcomes.

  • 08
    Automated confidence

    192 tests covering protocols, audio, failure paths, and infrastructure.
