SECTION IV / BUILDER'S NOTES

The difficult parts are between the services.

This project is less about connecting APIs than preserving timing, isolation, and trust while a live conversation is moving through them.

A.01

Full-duplex audio

Stateful μ-law/PCM conversion and bidirectional streaming without file containers.

A.02

Authenticated ingress

Exact-URL signature checks on Twilio webhook and WebSocket boundaries.

A.03

Versioned personas

Prompts and voices are configured in DynamoDB and snapshotted per call.

A.04

Bounded pipelines

Queue limits, backpressure, bounded retries, and idempotent terminal cleanup.

A.05

Privacy-aware signals

CloudWatch logs and metrics omit phone numbers, raw audio, and transcript text.

A.06

Public-demo controls

Caller rate, global concurrency, duration, and rolling budget safeguards.

A.07

Durable lifecycle

On-demand persistence with deterministic outcomes and retention controls.

A.08

Automated confidence

192 tests cover protocol drift, audio, failure paths, and infrastructure contracts.
