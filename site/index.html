04 ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Reliability is designed into the route.

01

Full-duplex audio

Stateful μ-law/PCM conversion preserves frame continuity in both directions.

02

Authenticated streaming ingress

Twilio signatures, exact public URLs, Account SID, and media contracts fail closed.

03

Bounded failure isolation

Per-call queues, backpressure, retries, and cleanup prevent one call from destabilizing another.

04

Public-demo safeguards

Caller rate, global concurrency, call duration, rolling budget, and transcript controls bound exposure.
